I’m Anne-Lynn, a graphic designer who loves turning ideas into things people can see, hold, and understand.

My work centres on visual communication: posters, signage, packaging, branding, and print pieces that help people understand things at a glance. Whether it’s guiding someone through a space, explaining a big idea on a single poster, or helping a product stand out on the shelf, I care about design that’s both beautiful and useful.

I’m always excited to work with people who have something to say. If you have a project in mind, I’d love to hear about it.
