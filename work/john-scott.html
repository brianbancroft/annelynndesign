Candidate Signage

The sign had to say who John is in a couple of seconds.
Deep green and bold type show he’s practical and competent. The gold name and his smile keep him approachable. A maple leaf and “For Ladysmith Council” make it clear he’s a neighbour, not a career politician.

Industry:
Local government & politics
Service:
Campaign signage, rack card & print advertising

Did you see the sign?

Placed along busy local roads, the sign had to be readable for drivers in vehicles. It was designed so that even from a long way off, the word “Scott” stands out, friendly and approachable enough to draw drivers in closer to see his face and first name.

Rack Card

The rack card had to carry the same approachable feel at first glance while also providing the details of John’s platform. The front leads with John’s portrait alongside a short personal message, and the back gives his four clear priorities so that voters can quickly see what he stands for.

Magazine Ad

This same look and feel carries well into print meaning that voters can recognize John at a glance.
