The Box Design and Look

The shower caps were packaged in premium boxes with a clear PVC window and convenient touch hole, delivering an upscale, gift-worthy presentation. Clear communication was essential — shoppers instantly recognize it as a shower cap.

Creating the Right Look

The use of a PVC window was important for showing off the pattern and allowing the customer to feel the fabric. In order for the customer to want the product, they had to imagine themselves wearing it.

Before and After

The original PVC pouch hid the product’s identity — it was nearly impossible to tell it was a shower cap. By redesigning it into a premium gift-style box with a clear PVC window and convenient touch hole, the shower cap now communicates quality and style at first glance.

The Line-Up

This product is sold in many different patterns and colours that rotate on a quarterly basis. Each shower cap has a unique pattern and colour scheme.
