The Box Design and Look

The shower caps were packaged in premium boxes with a clear PVC window and convenient touch hole, delivering an upscale, gift-worthy presentation that grabs the attention of the shopper.

Clear communication was essential—shoppers instantly recognize it as a shower cap. Each unique pattern and colour palette is enhanced by bright, complementary packaging hues that make every design stand out.

Creating the Right Look

When I received this project, I knew then that using a PVC window to show off the shower cap's pattern was the best starting point. These designs helped me figure out that in order for the customer to want the product, they had to imagine themselves wearing it.

Before and After

The original PVC pouch hid the product’s identity — it was nearly impossible to tell it was a shower cap, much less an appealing one. By redesigning it into a premium gift-style box with a clear PVC window and convenient touch hole, the shower cap now communicates quality and style at first glance.

The Line-Up

This product is sold in many different patterns and colours that rotate on a quarterly basis. Each shower cap has a special pattern and colour scheme partnered with it.
