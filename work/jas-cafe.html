Packaging

JAS Cafe wanted to take their wontons beyond the restaurant and sell them frozen at local markets. I designed bold, eye-catching labels for resealable bags, so they stand out on a crowded market table and look just as good in a home freezer.

Signage

Signage advertising frozen wontons. These were sold at local markets, events and in the restaurant.
