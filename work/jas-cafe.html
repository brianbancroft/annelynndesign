Jas Cafe menu

The Jas Cafe Story

Jas Cafe wanted a menu design that felt warm and authentic, reflecting real Chinese comfort food while staying easy for new diners to navigate.

Industry
Restaurant
Service
Menu, packaging and signage

Menu

The main menu needed to balance traditional Chinese dishes with clear, scannable categories so new guests could order with confidence.

Catering Menu

A separate catering menu highlights shareable, family-style dishes and packaging-friendly options for large group orders.

Sandwich Board

The sandwich board needed to stand out from the street, using bold type and a simplified colour palette that reads clearly at a glance.

Packaging

Takeout containers, bags, and napkins all needed the same brand treatment as the menu, so the experience feels cohesive from table to takeout.

Signage

Exterior and interior signage needed to be visible from the street while echoing the same warm colour palette and typography used throughout the menu.
