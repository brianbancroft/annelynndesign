HI! I'm Anne-Lynn, a graphic designer who helps businesses turn complex ideas into clear, engaging, and memorable visuals.

Originally from the Ottawa River Valley and now based on Vancouver Island, I've been designing print materials, custom packaging, and brand experiences since 2008.

Over the years I've worked on in-house marketing teams, at design agencies, and with my own clients, and that mix has taught me how to balance creative ideas with real business goals. I work closely with businesses and organizations to build distinct brand identities, refresh their marketing materials, and make sure everything they put out looks and sounds consistent.

Whether I'm creating brand guidelines from scratch or giving existing materials new life, my goal is the same: design that connects with the right people and keeps working long after launch.

If you'd love to chat about a project or have any questions, please reach out.
